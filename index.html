( 02 ) About

A Berlin gallery and laboratory that re-imagines art, science and society through sound.

A Across disciplines

Artists, technologists, scientists, designers and researchers working in the same room.

B Living laboratories

Evolving installations, visible signal flows, spatial audio, tactile interfaces and open prototyping — experiments over finished works.

C You're part of it

Exhibitions, performances and public feedback sessions that treat sound as a tool for shaping spaces and collective experience.

D Open methods

We publish how we work so others can reuse and adapt it — sound as a shared protocol for inclusive cultural and technological innovation.
